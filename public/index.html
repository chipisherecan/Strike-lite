How to edit maps

Maps are built from brushes (solid boxes and ramps), like CS 1.6 maps in Hammer. The editor opens in 3D; the Top-down 2D button switches to the view from above, where up on the screen is north.

  • 3D view: hold the right mouse button to look, with WASD to fly, Q/E down/up, Shift fast. Wheel moves forward and back, middle-drag pans, F goes to the selection.
  • Select in 3D: click a part. Floors made of several pieces select whole; Alt+click takes one piece, Shift+click adds more.
  • Move (1): drag an arrow, or a square between arrows. Resize (2): drag a yellow square to move that side, including the top and bottom. Both follow the grid snap; hold Shift to ignore it.
  • 2D view: right-drag to pan, mouse wheel to zoom.
  • Select (S): click a brush, Shift+click for more, drag on empty space to box-select. Drag to move, drag the yellow squares to resize. Arrow keys nudge, Page Up/Down raise/lower.
  • Box (B): pick a preset (wall, floor/platform, crate, roof), set bottom/top heights and textures, then drag a rectangle.
  • Ramp (R): set the low and high heights and which way it rises, then drag a rectangle. Use ramps for stairs and slopes.
  • Zones (Z): drag where bombsite A/B and the T/CT spawns are.
  • Decor (D): lamps (under roofs), palms, trees, doors (drag across a doorway), awnings.
  • Light (L): click to add a light that is baked into the map lighting.
  • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo/redo, Ctrl+D duplicate, Delete remove.
  • Players can walk up 0.45 m steps and jump about 0.95 m. Doorways need at least 2 m of headroom.
  • Check tests that both teams can reach both sites. Save map stores it for every new lobby (press F5 afterwards). Reset to original brings back the traced map.
